# AI-Sugarcane-Field-Research

A research methodology project exploring the scope of AI, remote sensing, and IoT-based approaches in sugarcane field agriculture. This repository documents our group's literature review, identified research gaps, and proposed research direction.

## 📌 About

Sugarcane is one of the most economically important crops globally, but its cultivation faces challenges like water stress, soil salinity, pest/disease attack, and difficulty in large-scale field monitoring. This project reviews recent IEEE and journal papers to understand how AI, machine learning, remote sensing, and IoT are being applied to solve these problems — and identifies where the gaps still are.

## 🔍 Key Areas Covered

- Precision Agriculture using AI & Remote Sensing
- Satellite-based Yield Prediction (vegetation indices, ML regression models)
- Computer Vision for Plant/Stem Counting (Faster R-CNN, RT-DETR)
- Sugarcane Disease Detection (CNN, SVM, attention-based deep learning)
- IoT & UAV-based Smart Irrigation and Monitoring
- Dataset Availability and Model Generalization Challenges

## 🧠 Research Gaps Identified

- Limited generalization across regions/climates due to small, region-specific datasets
- Poor model robustness under real field conditions (lighting, occlusion)
- Lack of low-cost, farmer-friendly, multi-language solutions
- Fragmented systems — yield prediction, disease detection, and irrigation rarely integrated
- Scarcity of centralized, standardized public datasets
- Limited focus on model explainability (e.g., Grad-CAM)

## 🎯 Proposed Direction

Building a lightweight, real-time decision-support framework that integrates satellite data, low-cost IoT sensors, and an edge-optimized deep learning model for combined yield prediction and disease detection — with explainability built in.

## 🏷️ Keywords

Precision Agriculture, Remote Sensing, Vegetation Indices (NDVI/GNDVI), Yield Prediction, CNN, SVM, Faster R-CNN, RT-DETR, IoT, UAV, Smart Irrigation, Disease Classification, Explainable AI, Edge Computing
